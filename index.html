Identify aircraft

POSSIBLE ONLY
Aircraft observed near both airports in the scheduled time windows appear first; live route candidates appear below them. Historical observations are stronger evidence but are not airline-confirmed assignments. Live search uses FlyItalyADSB, ADSB.lol and ADSB.fi.
The app does not derive or compare callsigns with public flight numbers. It uses route geometry, heading, timing, aircraft type and the ADS-B operator label to shortlist traffic. A shortlist is not proof of the aircraft assigned to your flight: the free feeds do not provide a reliable public-flight-number-to-registration assignment. Check the registration before selecting.
